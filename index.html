One tool, two paths.

Foundry adapts its setup experience and what it generates based on how you work.

Independent developer

Personal projects · Freelance · Learning · Hobby

A streamlined setup that stays out of your way. The interview asks about your personal workflow and pain points, and Foundry builds a lean team that matches how you actually work.

  • Shorter wizard, no compliance overhead
  • Interview focused on your velocity and common mistakes
  • Behavior presets tuned for solo development
  • Free, local knowledge graph and memory system

Team or organization

Shared codebases · Team standards · Compliance requirements

Upload your policy documents and Foundry extracts enforceable rules from them. Every generated agent, hook, and permission traces back to the source doc that required it.

  • Standards & policy extraction from your own docs
  • Compliance frameworks: SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, GDPR, ISO 27001
  • Enforcement artifacts (permissions, rules, hooks) with full traceability
  • Portable team blueprints — share a config across projects

What you get

The building blocks — and the intelligence that ties them together.

◆AI-designed agents

The proposal engine reads your code, asks focused questions, and designs agents matched to your stack and conventions — not generic templates.

⎔Graphify knowledge graph

A local semantic graph of your codebase, updated on every commit. Agents query it instead of reading raw files — ~71× fewer tokens. Free forever, runs entirely offline.

❋MemPalace memory

Architectural decisions, conventions, and pain points accumulate across sessions. The Historian files new knowledge; every agent can search it.

▣Behavior presets & toggles

Plan-before-acting, conventional commits, protection warnings, standing-order gates. Presets bundle sensible defaults; per-toggle overrides let you fine-tune.

◎2×2 agent taxonomy

Every agent has a role (Specialist or Guardian) and a kind (Worker or Scholar). Consistent mental model across the roster — visible in every surface.

▶Open architecture

Everything Foundry generates is standard Claude Code configuration — plain .md files, hooks, settings.json. No lock-in.
